## 2. Vision

> **To be the most trusted luxury aesthetic and wellness destination for women in New Cairo — where medical excellence meets a transformative, private, and elegant experience.**

Revera is not competing on price. Revera is competing on feeling, experience, and visible results. The long-term vision is to become the name that upper-class women in New Cairo associate with confidence, elegance, and self-care — the way they associate a luxury hotel with a certain quality of living.

---

## 3. Mission

> **To deliver science-backed aesthetic and medical care through a deeply personal, luxury experience — giving every woman who walks through our doors the confidence she deserves.**

Every decision at Revera — from the marble reception to the WhatsApp response tone — must serve this mission. The patient experience begins the moment she sees an Instagram post and ends long after she walks out the door glowing.

## 4. Brand Positioning

### Positioning Statement

*"We don't sell sessions… we sell confidence, elegance, and transformation."*

Revera is a premium aesthetic polyclinic for women who value elegance, privacy, and visible transformation — delivered through expert medical care and a luxury experience at every touchpoint.

### What We Are

- A luxury medical destination, not a clinical facility
- A confidence-building experience, not a treatment provider
- A trusted authority in aesthetic and women's health, not a discount clinic
- An exclusive space for women who want the best, not the cheapest

### What We Are Not

- A high-volume, low-margin clinic
- A promotional, discount-driven brand
- A generic "medical clinic" aesthetic
- Loud, pushy, or salesy in any communication

### Competitive Positioning

| Axis | Revera | Typical competitor |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Premium — never discounted | Promotional, frequent offers |
| Volume | Limited, curated client slots | High turnover |
| Experience | Luxury from first scroll to afterglow | Clinical and transactional |
| Trust signal | Doctor credibility + real results | Price and availability |
| Communication | Soft, elegant, editorial | Hard sell, urgency-driven |

## 5. Target Audience

### Primary Persona — "Her"

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | 23–45 |
| Location | New Cairo, 5th Settlement, Rehab, Madinaty |
| Class | Upper and upper-middle class |
| Interests | Skincare, aesthetics, self-care, fashion, wellness |
| Platforms | Instagram (primary), TikTok (secondary), WhatsApp (conversion) |
| Decision drivers | Before/after results, doctor credibility, clinic environment, word of mouth |

### Her Mindset

- *"I don't want cheap — I want the best."*
- Influenced heavily by Instagram aesthetics and visual proof
- Trusts before/after results more than any ad copy
- Doctor credibility is non-negotiable — she researches before booking
- The physical space must feel luxury — it signals the quality of care
- Privacy matters: she doesn't want to feel like a number in a queue
- She buys confidence and transformation, not a procedure name

### Secondary Persona

Women aged 28–55 referred by existing clients. Word of mouth and UGC are primary acquisition channels for this group. Trust is already partially established — the job is to confirm it.

## 6. Brand Identity

### 6.1 Brand Pillars

These five pillars govern every decision — visual, verbal, experiential.

| Pillar | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Luxury Experience** | Every touchpoint — online and offline — feels premium. The reception, the WhatsApp reply, the Instagram caption, the packaging. Nothing cheap. Nothing generic. |
| **Medical Trust & Expertise** | Science-backed, doctor-led care. The doctor's face, credentials, and voice are central to all content. Results are real, not filtered. |
| **Real Transformation** | Visible, honest, compelling results. Before/after content is our strongest conversion asset — it must be authentic and high quality. |
| **Femininity & Confidence** | We celebrate women at their best. The tone is empowering, never patronizing. We talk to her as an equal, not as a patient. |
| **Soft Selling** | We never push. We never discount. We create desire through aspiration and prove value through results. The offer comes last, after trust is built. |

### 6.2 Brand Personality

If Revera were a person, she would be:

- A 35-year-old woman who is accomplished, elegant, and unhurried
- She speaks with quiet confidence — never loud, never urgent
- She is warm but not casual; professional but not cold
- She has excellent taste and does not need to announce it
- She is the friend who always looks effortlessly put-together and knows exactly who to recommend

**In three words:** Elegant · Confident · Trustworthy

**Not:** Flashy · Pushy · Generic

### 6.3 Brand Archetypes

**Primary:** The Caregiver — devoted to the wellbeing and transformation of her clients  
**Secondary:** The Ruler — premium, authoritative, the standard others are measured against

## 7. Visual Identity

### 7.1 Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Olive Green | `#414E36` | Main headings, dominant icons, primary CTAs, brand anchor |
| Dark | Deepest Olive-Black | `#1F251A` | Alert bars, high-contrast text, dark panels |
| Secondary | Muted Sage Olive | `#5A6A51` | Descriptions, subtitles, body text, bullet points |
| Sand | Sophisticated Sand | `#F2EFE9` | Dividers, card borders, form inputs |
| Background | Luxurious Warm Ivory | `#FBFBF9` | Primary screen background — never cold white |
| Tint | Sage Tint Canvas | `#EDF1EC` | Bento grids, highlighted cards, alternating sections |
| Accent | Warm Royal Gold | `#C4AE7C` | Gold emblem highlights, star ratings, VIP indicators — use sparingly |

**Color rules:**
- Never use pure white `#FFFFFF` as a background — always use warm ivory `#FBFBF9`
- Gold `#C4AE7C` is a luxury signal — use it deliberately and sparingly. Never as a background fill.
- Never introduce colors outside this palette without brand approval

### 7.2 Typography

**Display / Headings:** Serif — elegant, editorial weight. Evokes established luxury.  
**Body / UI:** Clean sans-serif — readable, modern, clinical precision.  
**Arabic:** Match the weight and warmth of the Latin type selections. Never use a generic system Arabic font.

**Typography rules:**
- Headlines speak in brand voice — confident, short, declarative
- Body text is calm and informative — never cluttered
- Never use more than two typefaces in one layout
- Arabic and English must feel visually harmonious when displayed together

### 7.3 Photography Style

**Overall direction:** European wellness editorial — warm, clean, aspirational. Think Augustinus Bader, Clinique La Prairie. Not a Dubai clinic, not a medical ad.

| Subject | Direction |
|---|---|
| Women / clients | Early 30s–40s, glowing skin, serene confidence, natural expression. Never stock-photo smiling. |
| Doctor | Female, professional, warm genuine smile, clean skin, white coat, blurred clinic background. Authoritative but approachable. |
| Clinic interior | Marble, walnut wood, gold accents, warm lighting. Never fluorescent. Always clean — no cables, no clutter. |
| Treatments | Elegant hands, clean close-ups. Never graphic or clinical-looking. |
| Before/After | Consistent lighting, same angle, neutral expression in both shots. Labeled with treatment name and session count. |

**Photography don'ts:**
- No stock photos of call center agents or generic medical imagery
- No cold blue clinical lighting
- No visible cables, equipment stickers, or power sockets in hero shots
- No heavy makeup or overly edited skin in aspirational images
- No loud, cluttered compositions — always clean negative space

### 7.4 Video Style

- Slow motion where possible — cinematic, deliberate
- Warm color grading matching the brand palette
- No loud music — ambient, refined audio
- No fast cuts or promotional-style editing
- Reels open with a visual hook, not a title card

### 7.5 Logo Usage

- The Revera gold logo on the backlit marble panel is the primary brand mark
- Never place the logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Never distort, recolor, or add effects to the logo
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "R"
- On digital: use the dark olive version on light backgrounds, the gold version on dark backgrounds

## 8. Brand Voice & Tone

### 8.1 Voice Principles

| Principle | What it sounds like | What it does NOT sound like |
|---|---|---|
| Confident | "Limited consultations available this month." | "Book NOW before it's too late!!!" |
| Warm | "We're here whenever you're ready." | "Don't miss out on this deal." |
| Expert | "Our doctors tailor every treatment to your skin's specific needs." | "We do Botox and fillers." |
| Exclusive | "Luxury Skin Package — accepted by invitation or appointment." | "50% off this week only." |
| Empowering | "You deserve to feel like the best version of yourself." | "Fix your skin problems today." |

### 8.2 Tone by Channel

| Channel | Tone |
|---|---|
| Instagram captions | Editorial, soft, poetic. Short sentences. Breathing room. No emojis in excess. |
| Instagram Stories | Slightly warmer and more personal — behind the scenes, polls, real moments. |
| WhatsApp (first reply) | Warm welcome, personal address, premium — never robotic or template-sounding. |
| WhatsApp (follow-up) | Gentle scarcity, soft close. Never pushy. |
| Website headlines | Short, declarative, aspirational. Arabic and English feel equally premium. |
| Website body | Informative, calm, credible. Doctor language where needed, not jargon. |
| Paid ads | Problem-first (her pain, not our service) → results → soft CTA. |

### 8.3 Language Rules

**Always say:**
- "Luxury Skin Package — Limited Clients Monthly"
- "VIP Consultation — By Appointment Only"
- "Exclusive Seasonal Experience"
- "We tailor every session to you"

**Never say:**
- "50% off"
- "Cheap" / "Affordable" / "Budget-friendly"
- "Book NOW"
- "Don't miss out"
- "We are the best clinic in Cairo" (show, don't tell)

### 8.4 Arabic Voice

The Arabic voice must feel like a knowledgeable, elegant older sister — warm, personal, slightly formal but never stiff. Never use slang that undermines the luxury positioning. The Arabic tone should match the English tone: confident, warm, premium.

**Example WhatsApp opening (Arabic):**
> أهلاً بيكي في Revera 💫 حابين نرشحلك أفضل تجربة تناسب بشرتك مع دكاترتنا

**Example scarcity message (Arabic):**
> الأماكن المتاحة الأسبوع ده محدودة جداً

**Example close (Arabic):**
> تحبي نحجزلك مع الدكتور؟ 🌿

## 9. Content Strategy

### 9.1 Content Goal

Every piece of content — organic or paid — must do one or more of three things:

1. **Build Desire** — make her want to be there
2. **Build Trust** — make her believe in the results and the doctors
3. **Drive Action** — create the feeling of "I need to go there — now"

### 9.2 Content Mix

| Type | % of Content | Goal | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authority | 30% | Trust | Doctor-to-camera, skincare education, myth vs. fact |
| Transformation | 25% | Conversion | Before/after, real cases, testimonials |
| Lifestyle / Luxury | 20% | Desire | Clinic vibes, client experience, interior details |
| Engagement | 15% | Interaction | Polls, sliders, relatable skin-concern content |
| Soft Offers | 10% | Premium positioning | Exclusive packages, limited slots — never discounts |

### 9.3 Monthly Content Cadence

| Format | Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Reels | 3 | ~12 |
| Stories | 2 per day | ~60 |
| Carousels | 1 | ~4 |

### 9.4 Sample Weekly Schedule

| Day | Content |
|---|---|
| Monday | Reel: Before / After transformation |
| Wednesday | Reel: Doctor skincare tip or education |
| Friday | Reel: Clinic vibe / lifestyle |
| Weekend | Carousel: Skin mistakes or skincare guide + BTS Stories + Polls |

### 9.5 Content Don'ts

- Never post price comparisons or discount announcements
- Never use stock imagery of generic medical or call center settings
- Never over-filter before/after results — authenticity is the asset
- Never post without a clear content goal (desire / trust / action)
- Never let posting consistency drop — the algorithm rewards regularity

## 10. Paid Advertising Strategy

### 10.1 Platforms

**Primary:** Meta (Instagram + Facebook)  
**Secondary:** TikTok

### 10.2 Three-Phase Funnel

```
Phase 1: Awareness (Month 1–2)
→ Reach and engage cold audiences
→ Reels: before/after, problem-based videos
→ Hook: her pain point, not our service
→ "Why isn't your skin improving despite all those products?"

Phase 2: Lead Capture (Month 2–3)
→ Drive WhatsApp and message leads
→ Client testimonials filmed with intention
→ Doctor explainer: breaking down solutions to her exact concerns

Phase 3: Retargeting (Ongoing)
→ Convert warmed audiences into bookings
→ "Book Now" — direct, frictionless CTA
→ "Limited consultation slots" — urgency without desperation
→ Free consultation as low-barrier first step
```

### 10.3 Ad Creative Rules

- Lead with her pain or aspiration — never with the service name
- Use real before/after content as the highest-converting creative
- Never run a discount offer as a paid ad — it signals desperation
- Retargeting ads can use soft scarcity: "Limited slots this month"

### 10.4 KPIs

| Metric | What it measures |
|---|---|
| CPM (Cost Per Message) | Primary paid ads efficiency — how cheaply we reach warm leads |
| CVR (Message → Booking) | The conversion rate that drives revenue |
| CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) | True cost of each new client acquired |

## 11. Influencer Strategy

### 11.1 The Right Profile

- **Followers:** 10K–100K (micro-influencers only)
- **Location:** Based in New Cairo / 5th Settlement
- **Aesthetic:** Authentic, classy, elegant — not cheap or overly commercial
- **Audience:** Must match Revera's target persona

> Smaller reach with precise targeting outperforms mass reach every time in luxury markets.

### 11.2 The Non-Negotiable Rule

The influencer must embody the brand. Classy, aspirational, and genuinely aligned with Revera's values — not just a large follower count. A misaligned influencer can damage the brand positioning permanently.

### 11.3 Campaign Formats

| Format | Description |
|---|---|
| Experience Visit | Full journey filmed — arrival, consultation, treatment, afterglow. Shows the entire luxury experience end-to-end. |
| Transformation Story | Before → After. Her honest story in her own words. The highest-converting influencer format. |

## 12. WhatsApp Conversion Protocol

Every lead that messages Revera must feel welcomed into a premium experience — not processed through a generic funnel.

### Step 1 — Warm Welcome

> أهلاً بيكي في Revera 💫 حابين نرشحلك أفضل تجربة تناسب بشرتك مع دكاترتنا

**Tone:** Warm, personal, premium. She should feel like a VIP guest, not a sales lead.

### Step 2 — Gentle Scarcity

> الأماكن المتاحة الأسبوع ده محدودة جداً

**Why it works:** Creates urgency without pressure. Reinforces exclusivity — Revera is in demand.

### Step 3 — Frictionless Close

> تحبي نحجزلك مع الدكتور؟ 🌿

**Why it works:** One simple question. Remove all friction. Make booking feel like the natural, easy next step.

## 13. Website Strategy

### 13.1 Core Principles

| Principle | Standard |
|---|---|
| Premium first impression | Clean luxury design that communicates credibility in under 3 seconds |
| Conversion-first | Every page moves her toward a consultation booking |
| Speed is non-negotiable | A slow site is an expensive brand mistake — target under 2.5s load time |
| Mobile-first | The primary audience browses on iPhone — the mobile experience is the experience |

### 13.2 Required Pages & Sections

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Hero | Single aspirational image + headline + WhatsApp CTA — no rotating carousel |
| Services | One photo per category, dermatology featured prominently |
| Before / After | Authenticated, labeled, consent-noted results gallery |
| Doctor profiles | One canonical portrait per doctor, credentials, warm biography |
| Testimonials | Real client words — video preferred over text |
| Clinic interior | 3–4 curated interior shots — reception, treatment room, detail |
| Stats / Trust bar | Numbers that signal scale: sessions delivered, years of experience, satisfaction rate |
| WhatsApp CTA | Always visible — floating button on mobile, persistent in footer |

### 13.3 Website Photo Rules

- Hero: single image only — no carousel
- Doctor portraits: one canonical photo used consistently across all sections
- No stock photos of generic medical or office settings
- Before/after: always include treatment label and written consent notice
- All images: warm ivory or sage tones — no cold clinical blue lighting

## 14. User-Generated Content (UGC)

> "People don't trust brands. They trust people."

UGC transforms real clients into Revera's most credible ambassadors — organically.

### UGC Formats

| Format | Description |
|---|---|
| Post-session selfie video | Filmed right after the treatment — raw, glowing, authentic. Captures the emotion of transformation at its peak. |
| "My Experience at Revera" | Full storytelling format — from her first DM to walking out transformed. Highly shareable. |
| Honest reaction | Unscripted, real moments. The imperfection makes it credible — and credibility converts. |

### How to Encourage UGC

- Create a photogenic moment in the clinic (the branded wall, the illuminated logo, the marble reception)
- Train staff to suggest a post-session photo in a natural, non-pushy way
- Feature client content on Stories immediately — social proof loop

## 18. Brand Checklist — Before Publishing Anything

Use this checklist before any content, ad, or communication goes live.

- [ ] Does it look luxury? (clean, uncluttered, warm tones)
- [ ] Does it sound like Revera? (confident, warm, never pushy)
- [ ] Does it build desire, trust, or drive action?
- [ ] Is the image real and authentic — not stock or over-filtered?
- [ ] If it has an offer — is it framed as exclusive, not discounted?
- [ ] Is the Arabic copy as premium as the English?
- [ ] Would the target client (35, upper class, New Cairo) feel this was made for her?
- [ ] Has it been checked for spelling, Arabic grammar, and brand tone?

If any answer is no — revise before publishing.
